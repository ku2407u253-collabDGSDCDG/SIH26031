## Quality assessment and grading of onions are often subjective and vary across procurement centers, resulting in disputes and inconsistencies.

 - 📱 AI-Powered Mobile App — Capture onion images using a smartphone for instant quality assessment.
 
 - 🔍 AI Defect Detection — Detect rotten, damaged, sprouted, stained and undersized onions 

 - 📏 Automated Size & Shape Assessment — Use image segmentation + calibration to estimate onion diameter

 - 🏷️ Automated Grading Engine — Apply official Grade-A / URS quality parameters to generate a consistent grading recommendation.

 - 🔐 Transparent & Traceable Results — Generate a digital report with images, confidence scores and grading details, reducing human bias, disputes and delays.
